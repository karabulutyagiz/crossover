Game modes

Six ways to play

They all test the same knowledge from different angles. Club pairs are the warm-up; the letter mode is the exam.

Club × Club

The core mode. Two clubs are drawn and the first player to name someone who wore both shirts takes the round. Free.

Country × Club

A nation and a club. Name a player from that country who turned out for that club.

Social Pack

Letter × Club

A letter and a club. The answer has to start with that letter. The hardest mode in the game.

Social Pack

Bot match

Practice with no trophies at stake. Easy, Medium and Hard opponents.

Ranked

Match against a live opponent, win trophies, climb from Local Pitch to the GOAT arena.

Friendlies

Add a friend, send an invite or share a room code. No trophies — just bragging rights.

Country × Club and Letter × Club unlock with the Social Pack and must be active for both players to match.

Ranked

Climb from Local Pitch to GOAT

Every ranked win earns trophies and pushes you towards the next arena, where opponents get sharper. Knowledge is enough at the bottom; at the top you need speed too.

Pick a mode, take the pitch

Club × Club is free. The rest depends on how bold you are.
