Game modes

CrossOver Football game modes

Shared-player duels, football tic-tac-toe, anagrams, country and letter challenges, plus the daily career puzzle. Open a card for rules and sample questions.

Club × Club

The core mode. Two clubs are drawn and the first player to name someone who wore both shirts takes the round. Free.

Football Tic-Tac-Toe

Find a footballer who meets both clues in a cell. Complete a row, column or diagonal on the 3 × 3 board before your opponent.

Social Pack

Football Anagrams

Solve the scrambled player name in Çöz Kazan. Seven timed rounds put your football memory and word skills to the test.

Social Pack

Country × Club

A nation and a club. Name a player from that country who turned out for that club.

Social Pack

Letter × Club

A letter and a club. The answer has to start with that letter. The hardest mode in the game.

Social Pack

Daily Career Quiz

Clubs are revealed one at a time. Identify the player from their career path within four guesses.

Club × Club is free. Football Tic-Tac-Toe, Çöz Kazan, Country × Club and Letter × Club require the Social Pack; both players need access to match in these modes.

Modes versus match types

A mode determines the question. Ranked play is the trophy competition; matchmaking may fall back to a bot. Bot matches offer Easy, Medium and Hard practice. Friendlies use an invitation or room code and award no trophies or XP.

Ranked

Climb from Local Pitch to GOAT

Every ranked win earns trophies and pushes you towards the next arena, where opponents get sharper. Knowledge is enough at the bottom; at the top you need speed too.
