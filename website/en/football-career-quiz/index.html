How Daily Career works

  1. Open Daily Career in the app. Everyone receives the same footballer for the day.
  2. The first club clue is visible at the start. Use the club and displayed years to make a guess.
  3. A wrong guess uses one attempt and reveals the next club. You have four guesses in total.
  4. Your daily attempt ends when you guess correctly or run out of guesses. A new career arrives on the next Istanbul calendar day.
  5. Share the result and compare how many clues you needed with your friends.

Which career clues are most useful?

Do not treat the club name in isolation. The displayed year separates players from different eras at the same club. When another stop appears, test your first guess against it: if the player does not connect both clubs, think again.

Look beyond the club most associated with a star. Early-career clubs and short spells can be useful clues. For well-travelled players, connecting two neighbouring stops is often easier than recalling an entire career at once.

Frequently asked questions

How many guesses do I get in the career quiz?
Daily Career gives you four guesses. A wrong answer uses an attempt and reveals the next club clue.
Does everyone get the same footballer?
Yes. All players solve the same daily career during the same Istanbul calendar day.
When does the daily career reset?
The question resets at the start of a new day in Istanbul time. The app displays the next reset time.
Is today’s answer on this page?
This page contains the rules and a separate learning example. The actual daily question and its result are in the app’s Daily Career screen.
