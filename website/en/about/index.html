About

Meet CrossOver Football: shared-player duels, football tic-tac-toe and player anagrams. Learn about the game, free access and how to contact us.

From football conversation to competition

“Who played for both clubs?” is the starting point for CrossOver Football. Club × Club asks you to find the shared footballer before your opponent. Football Tic-Tac-Toe adds board strategy; Çöz Kazan adds a race to solve scrambled player names.

Country × Club and Letter × Club offer different clues. In the daily career puzzle, identify the footballer as clubs are revealed. Find rules and worked examples in our game mode guides.

How do I start?

The iOS app and Club × Club are free, with guest sign-in available. Football Tic-Tac-Toe, Çöz Kazan, Country × Club and Letter × Club require the Social Pack. In-app purchases are optional. All play, including bot practice, requires an internet connection.

What is the web archive for?

The shared-player finder (Turkish) lists players recorded at both selected clubs. Use the archive to practise between matches. Our data sources page explains its scope and limitations.

Contact CrossOver Football

For game support, data corrections or content collaborations, email info@crossoverfootball.com. Visit support for account and purchase questions.

CrossOver Football is an independent football knowledge game. References to clubs, leagues, players and data sources do not imply endorsement or official affiliation.
