FAQ

Common questions

What is CrossOver Football?

A football quiz game with shared-player duels, football tic-tac-toe, player anagrams, country and letter challenges, plus a daily career puzzle. Play against friends or practise with bots.

Is it free?

Yes. Downloading and playing Club × Club is free. Football Tic-Tac-Toe, Çöz Kazan, Country × Club and Letter × Club require the Social Pack. Optional in-app purchases are available.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The game is currently on iOS only; an Android build is in preparation.

Do loan and youth spells count?

Recorded loan spells and academy records merged into the parent club can count. This checks career records, not senior match appearances. Read our data scope and correction policy.

What happens if I misspell a name?

Name matching is deliberately forgiving. Accents and common misspellings are normalised, so "snayder" still resolves to Wesley Sneijder.
