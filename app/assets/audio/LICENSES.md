All audio files under `assets/audio` are original synthetic/generated assets created for CrossOver Football in this implementation pass.

No third-party samples, real broadcast recordings, crowd recordings, player voice recordings, or Cristiano Ronaldo recordings are included.

## Asset Notes

- `music/mainMenu.wav`: original loopable electronic/percussion menu bed with light stadium texture.
- `ambience/stadiumAmbience.wav`: original synthetic low-level stadium-style noise bed.
- `ui/*.wav`: original short UI interaction SFX.
- `match/*.wav`: original countdown, answer, timer, match, and scoring SFX.
- `progression/*.wav`: original XP, trophy, reward, level-up, and arena unlock SFX.
- `social/*.wav`: original emote and notification SFX.
- `special/ronaldoCelebration.wav`: original synthetic "Siuuu-inspired" celebration-style chant. It is not a sample or imitation recording of Cristiano Ronaldo or any broadcast audio.
